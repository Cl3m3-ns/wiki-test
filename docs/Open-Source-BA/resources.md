# More Resources

## Open questions @AMPEL
- Maybe you find your information on our [official web presence](https://ampel-platform.eu).
- You can report your issue (question, bug report, feature request, improvement suggestion) here (==link==). To help us, please keep the following points in mind when creating an issue:
	- Use the search (in the wiki and in previous issues) before posting
	- Use a descriptive title
	- Describe the problem as accurately as possible
	- Make it reproducible
	- Include, if applicable, details, error messages and logs

## General information on medical devices
- The Johner Institute GmbH maintains a comprehensive wiki covering all international medical device regulations: https://blog.johner-institute.com/
- MDCG guidance (not legally binding, but MDR's de facto official interpretation): https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-sector/new-regulations/guidance-mdcg-endorsed-documents-and-other-guidance_en

## Templates
- The Open Regulatory GmbH provides open templates for your new QMS (quality management system): https://openregulatory.com/document_templates/document-list-qms
- Building on this, the Clinical Scientific Computing department at Guy's & St Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust has published additional QMS templates:: https://github.com/GSTT-CSC/QMS-Template
- Tidepool, the first OSS with FDA clearance, publishes its regulatory quality system as well. Two main distinctions should be noted. The documentation complies with FDA regulations (as opposed to the MDR), and this represents a full clearance in the sense of a CE-marked device. Requirements for in-house medical devices under the MDR are lower: https://www.tidepool.org/open
- For German-speaking contexts, you can use the [TMF template](https://www.toolpool-gesundheitsforschung.de/produkte/template-zur-dokumentation-von-datenschutz-folgenabschaetzungen-nach-art-35-dsgvo) for your Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA).
## Discussion spaces
- German only: The Medtec Online GmbH has been commissioned by the government to establish a discussion platform covering all topics related to medical devices: https://medteconline.de/
