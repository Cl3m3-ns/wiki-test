## 3. Providing the wiki through GitHub Pages

When a page is accessed, your browser connects to GitHub Pages servers. Technically necessary connection data is processed. GitHub specifically states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and may process other usage data, such as device and browser information, the date and time of the request, and the pages accessed. See the [GitHub Pages documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection).

This processing enables the wiki to be delivered reliably and securely and helps detect attacks and technical faults. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of this information service.

GitHub explains the scope, purpose, and duration of its data processing, as well as possible international data transfers, in its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The service providers are GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam, Netherlands, and GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

## 4. Search, cookies, and analytics

The wiki’s search function runs in the browser using statically provided page data. Search terms are not transmitted to an AMPEL Wiki server.

The AMPEL Wiki does not use its own analytics or marketing services. Information about cookies and similar technologies that GitHub may use as part of its services is available in [GitHub’s cookie policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-cookies).

## 5. Embedded YouTube videos

Some pages may embed YouTube videos in enhanced privacy mode via `www.youtube-nocookie.com`. When your browser loads such a video player, it connects to Google or YouTube. This may transmit your IP address, device and browser data, the date and time, and the address of the page visited. If you are signed in to Google, usage may be associated with your account.

According to YouTube, enhanced privacy mode prevents playback from being used to personalise the YouTube experience or advertising outside this wiki. It does not completely prevent data from being transferred to Google. Further information is available in the [YouTube help page on enhanced privacy mode](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780?hl=en) and [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).

## 6. External links

Simply displaying an external link does not transmit data to its destination. Data protection provisions of the respective external provider apply only when you follow the link. University Hospital Leipzig has no influence over their data processing.

## 7. Contact

If you contact us by email, the data you provide is processed to handle your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; if your enquiry concerns a contract or its performance, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR also applies. The data is deleted as soon as it is no longer required to handle the enquiry and no statutory retention obligations apply.

## 8. Your rights

Where the statutory requirements are met, you have the following rights in particular:

You may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. The lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal remains unaffected. To exercise your rights, contact [dsb@uniklinik-leipzig.de](mailto:dsb@uniklinik-leipzig.de).
